# Technical Risks

Likelihood / impact: **H**igh · **M**edium · **L**ow.

| #   | Risk                                                                                                                                                  | L   | I   | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Job source reliability** — endpoints change, go down, or return partial data                                                                        | H   | M   | Adapter isolation, recorded fixtures + drift tests, per-source health & auto-pause, raw payload retention for re-normalisation, multiple sources per market.                                                                                                         |
| 2   | **API availability / platform restrictions** — major job boards and professional networks prohibit automated access; public ATS APIs can be withdrawn | H   | H   | Only integrate sources with a documented access basis and ToS review; manual job entry as universal fallback; prioritise public ATS APIs and employer structured data; budget for a licensed aggregator API if coverage is insufficient. Never scrape against terms. |
| 3   | **AI hallucinations**                                                                                                                                 | H   | H   | Grounding protocol (facts with ids, mandatory citations, rejection of unknown ids), deterministic numeric/entity checks, QC agent on a different model, human approval, eval suite per prompt version.                                                               |
| 4   | **Resume inaccuracies / misrepresentation**                                                                                                           | M   | H   | Writers only see VERIFIED/USER_PROVIDED facts; AI_INFERRED can never become VERIFIED automatically; bullet-level source links; diff view; approval bound to content hash.                                                                                            |
| 5   | **Immigration information becomes outdated or is wrong**                                                                                              | H   | H   | Rules as versioned data with official sources and `last_verified_at`; staleness downgrades results to NEEDS_VERIFICATION; always framed as signals, not advice, with official links; periodic review task.                                                           |
| 6   | **Duplicate jobs**                                                                                                                                    | H   | M   | Fingerprint + near-duplicate detection, company resolution by domain, labelled evaluation sample, manual merge tool.                                                                                                                                                 |
| 7   | **Duplicate applications**                                                                                                                            | M   | H   | Unique `(user_id, job_id)`, company-level warning, idempotency keys on submit/send, `outbound_actions` ledger.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 8   | **Email deliverability** — sending from a user's Gmail can hurt their reputation or trigger spam filters                                              | M   | H   | Send only via the user's own mailbox through official API, low daily caps, personalised (not templated-bulk) content, no tracking pixels by default, bounce/complaint handling, no cold bulk outreach feature.                                                       |
| 9   | **OAuth failures / Google restricted-scope verification**                                                                                             | M   | H   | Minimal scopes, incremental consent, `NEEDS_REAUTH` handling, early start on Google verification (can take weeks and may require a security assessment); product works without Gmail (copy-to-clipboard / mailto fallback).                                          |
| 10  | **Workflow failures** — partial executions, stuck waits, duplicated side effects                                                                      | M   | H   | Persisted node state, idempotent handlers, approval gates, timeouts, dead-letter queue, retry-from-node, crash tests.                                                                                                                                                |
| 11  | **Security breach** — highly sensitive personal data + mailbox tokens                                                                                 | L   | H   | See security.md: encryption of tokens, authz matrix tests, least privilege, CSP, dependency hygiene, pen test before launch.                                                                                                                                         |
| 12  | **Privacy / GDPR non-compliance**                                                                                                                     | M   | H   | Data minimisation (no DOB/photo/special categories), export & deletion built in Phase 1, retention jobs, DPAs, EU data residency where possible, lawful-basis notes for contacts.                                                                                    |
| 13  | **AI cost overrun**                                                                                                                                   | M   | M   | Tiered routing (cheap models for extraction), caching by input hash (job analysis shared across users), per-user budgets with hard stop, batch jobs, cost telemetry per generation.                                                                                  |
| 14  | **Rate limits** (ours and upstream)                                                                                                                   | M   | M   | Per-source/per-host token buckets, honour `Retry-After`, queue concurrency limits, AI provider fallback.                                                                                                                                                             |
| 15  | **Scaling** — job catalog growth, vector search, analytics                                                                                            | L   | M   | Postgres indexing plan, materialised views, pgvector HNSW, partitioning `raw_job_postings` if needed; worker scales horizontally; monolith can split the worker/ingestion out later.                                                                                 |
| 16  | **Serverless limits** — long tasks timing out on Vercel                                                                                               | H   | M   | All long work in the worker process; web requests only enqueue.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 17  | **Prompt injection** via job descriptions, emails, websites                                                                                           | M   | H   | Delimited untrusted content, agents reading untrusted text have no side-effect tools, outputs validated, humans approve every outbound action.                                                                                                                       |
| 18  | **Vendor lock-in** (AI provider, auth library, hosting)                                                                                               | M   | M   | Provider abstraction, service layer independent of Next.js, standard Postgres, S3-compatible storage, containerisable worker.                                                                                                                                        |
| 19  | **Dependency churn** — fast-moving Next.js/Prisma majors (e.g. Prisma `latest` tag currently an 8.0 RC)                                               | M   | M   | Exact version pins for critical packages, lockfile, scheduled upgrade reviews, framework code confined to transports.                                                                                                                                                |
| 20  | **Scope creep / over-engineering**                                                                                                                    | M   | M   | Phase gates with explicit "not included" lists; tables and packages added only when used.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 21  | **Legal risk of automated applying** — employers or platforms may prohibit automated submissions                                                      | M   | H   | Submissions only via official APIs or by the user; human approval for each; no portal browser automation.                                                                                                                                                            |
| 22  | **Discrimination / bias** in matching or generated answers                                                                                            | L   | H   | No protected attributes collected; EEO questions never auto-answered; matching dimensions are explainable and user-weighted.                                                                                                                                         |
